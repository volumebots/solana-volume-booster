# Security Guidance

This repository documents a hosted product and does not contain the production ChartUp bot. Security reports may still concern impersonation, malicious links, copied bot identities, documentation errors, or vulnerabilities observed in an official ChartUp service.

## Reporting

Contact the verified support account [`@chartup_support`](https://t.me/chartup_support). Include:

- The affected ChartUp product or bot username
- A clear description of what happened
- The date and approximate time
- The blockchain network involved
- Relevant transaction hashes or public addresses
- Screenshots with private information removed

Do not publish an exploitable issue before ChartUp has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate it.

## Never Share

Do not send any person or bot:

- A private key
- A seed or recovery phrase
- Telegram login codes
- Passwords or authentication tokens
- Unredacted device or account secrets

The documented ChartUp workflow does not require wallet recovery information.

## Account Verification

Open bots through the [official link register](docs/OFFICIAL-LINKS.md), the [ChartUp website](https://www.chartup.io/), or the [official GitBook](https://chartup.gitbook.io/docs). Telegram search results and unsolicited messages are not proof of identity.
